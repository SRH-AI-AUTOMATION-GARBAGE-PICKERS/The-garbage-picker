# The Garbage Picker (Team Picker, ClawPi)

AI and Autonomous Systems Lab, SRH. Team Picker works with the ClawPi robot. Team Scout (MentorPi A1) finds the cubes, we fetch them.

## Product Goal (customer brief)
"I run a small warehouse. On an order, I want the robots to find the ordered cubes and bring them to the drop zone, without driving over anything and without being fooled by pictures. Scout finds, picker fetches."
